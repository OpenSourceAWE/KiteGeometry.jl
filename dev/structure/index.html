System definition

A SystemDefinition holds one awesIO structure document. Its component types are generated by src/build.jl from the schema vendored at src/awesio/structure_schema.yml from the awesIO commit gen/awesio.toml records, one field per required column, in the schema's order, each quantity's unit in its docstring. Blocks the schema does not name are kept in extras; a column it does not name is read only as a field of a registered model.

KiteGeometry.SystemDefinition — Type
SystemDefinition

A structure document: its metadata, one vector per table block, and the blocks the schema does not name.

  • metadata::Metadata: the document's metadata
  • points::Vector{Point}: the rows of points
  • segments::Vector{Segment}: the rows of segments
  • stations::Vector{Station}: the rows of stations
  • pulleys::Vector{Pulley}: the rows of pulleys
  • tethers::Vector{Tether}: the rows of tethers
  • winches::Vector{Winch}: the rows of winches
  • wings::Vector{Wing}: the rows of wings
  • canopy_faces::Vector{CanopyFace}: the rows of canopy_faces
  • bodies::Vector{Body}: the rows of bodies
  • tubes::Vector{Tube}: the rows of tubes
  • extras::OrderedDict{String, Any}: blocks the schema does not name, in the order read

KiteGeometry.Metadata — Type
Metadata

The metadata block of a structure document.

  • name::String
  • description::String
  • note::String
  • awesIO_version::String: Version of this schema the file was written against.
  • schema::String
  • n_points::Int: Number of rows in the points block.
  • connectivity_sha::String: Lowercase hex SHA-256 of the connectivity, so state recorded against one structure is not silently replayed against another that indexes differently. The preimage is ASCII and built in document order, in three sections: the number of points, then each segment's two endpoints as ONE-BASED row numbers into the points block; then the number of bodies, then each tube's two bodies as one-based row numbers into the bodies block; then the number of canopy faces, then each face's corners as row numbers into the points block. The numbers of one element are comma-separated and every field ends in a semicolon, so a lone number always opens a section. Three points joined in a row and spanned by one face, on two bodies joined by one tube, read 3;1,2;2,3;2;1,2;1;1,2,3;. Row numbers are one-based so that implementations in zero-based languages agree with each other; a reader that gets this wrong sees every file as a mismatch.

YAML and JSON

A document is read and written in either encoding, through SystemDefinition(document) and structure_document, whose docstrings say which awesIO_version and connectivity_sha each accepts and writes. What a round trip of a real system still loses is listed in 1-Bart-1/awesIO#3.

Components

KiteGeometry.Point — Type
Point{M}

A row of the points block. Mass points.

  • name::String: Unique name of the point. A writer whose geometry is index-keyed writes the index as a string, "1" upwards.
  • type::DynamicsType: STATIC: fixed in the world. DYNAMIC: free, moved by the forces on it. BODY_STATIC: fixed in the frame of the rigid body body names, moving with it and passing its force and moment on to it.
  • body::Union{Nothing, NameRef}: The rigid body a BODY_STATIC point is fixed to; null for any other point.
  • pos_ENU::SVector{3, Float64} [m]: Position in the world.
  • extra_mass::Float64 [kg]: Mass the point carries beyond its segments and canopy faces.
  • drag_area::Float64 [m^2]: Cross-sectional area the point's drag acts on.
  • drag_coefficient::Float64 [-]: Drag coefficient referred to drag_area.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel
source
KiteGeometry.Segment — Type
Segment{M}

A row of the segments block. Straight elastic elements between two points.

  • name::String: Unique name of the segment.
  • points::NTuple{2, NameRef}: The two points the segment joins.
  • l0::Float64 [m]: Unstretched length.
  • diameter::Float64 [m]: Diameter of the line, for its mass and drag.
  • density::Float64 [kg/m^3]: Density of the line's material.
  • unit_stiffness::Union{Float64, String} [N]: Axial stiffness times unstretched length, or the name of a nonlinear law.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel
source
KiteGeometry.Station — Type
Station{M}

A row of the stations block. Chordwise sections of a wing. A station's points give the local chord, from which a reader derives the section's angle of attack and, where the points trace the profile, its airfoil shape; the aerodynamic load of the span the station covers is put onto those points. A station's local twist is the angle its chord has turned through about the wing's KA y axis from where the document places it, positive pitching the leading edge up. A station is not tied to the aerodynamic mesh. A wing's stations run from +y to -y of its KA frame.

  • name::String: Unique name of the station.
  • wing::NameRef: The wing the station is a section of.
  • type::DynamicsType: DYNAMIC: the twist is a state, solved from the moments on the station. STATIC: the twist is a prescribed control input. KINEMATIC: the twist is prescribed by geometry, such as a flap hinged between two bodies.
  • points::Vector{NameRef}: The points the section is made of.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel
source

KiteGeometry.Tether — Type
Tether{M}

A row of the tethers block. Ordered runs of segments between two points. Material lives on the segments; a tether is the grouping a winch reels, and the length it is reeled from is its segments' l0.

  • name::String: Unique name of the tether.
  • start_point::NameRef: The point the tether starts at.
  • end_point::NameRef: The point the tether ends at.
  • segments::Vector{NameRef}: The tether's segments, in order from start_point to end_point.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel

KiteGeometry.Wing — Type
Wing{M}

A row of the wings block. Lifting surfaces, each with the stations that section it and at most one canopy, meshed by the canopy_faces that name the wing.

  • name::String: Unique name of the wing.
  • canopy_material::Union{Nothing, String}: The fabric every face of the wing's canopy is made of, or null where the wing has no canopy.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel

KiteGeometry.CanopyFace — Type
CanopyFace{M}

A row of the canopy_faces block. Triangles and quadrilaterals of fabric spanned between points, whose mass a reader shares among the corners. How the fabric carries load, as a membrane or as springs along its edges, is the reader's model.

  • name::String: Unique name of the face.
  • wing::NameRef: The wing whose canopy the face belongs to.
  • points::Vector{NameRef}: The face's three or four corners, in order around it.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel
source
KiteGeometry.Body — Type
Body{M}

A row of the bodies block. Rigid bodies. A body's KA frame is oriented as docs/source/conventions.rst defines _KA where the body has wing geometry, and as its writer likes where it has no leading edge.

  • name::String: Unique name of the body.
  • type::DynamicsType: DYNAMIC: free in six degrees of freedom. KINEMATIC: its pose is fitted to its points. STATIC: clamped to the world.
  • pos_ENU::SVector{3, Float64} [m]: The body's origin, the centre of its own mass, in the world.
  • Q_KA_to_ENU::SVector{4, Float64} [-]: Rotation from the body's KA frame into the world.
  • extra_mass::Float64 [kg]: Mass the body carries beyond the points fixed to it.
  • extra_inertia_KA::SMatrix{3, 3, Float64, 9} [kg*m^2]: Inertia tensor of extra_mass about the body's origin, in its KA axes. Principal moments are the case where the KA axes are the principal axes and the tensor is diagonal; a tool that carries no rotational inertia writes zeros.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel
source
KiteGeometry.Tube — Type
Tube{M}

A row of the tubes block. Inflated beam elements between two bodies, whose positions fix the element's ends and so its rest length. How it curves between those ends belongs to the element: only a shape its law cannot hold, or a taper, is a chain of tubes.

  • name::String: Unique name of the tube.
  • bodies::NTuple{2, NameRef}: The two bodies whose positions fix the tube's ends.
  • diameter::Float64 [m]: Diameter, one for the whole element.
  • pressure::Float64 [Pa]: Inflation pressure.
  • law::String: The stiffness law the element's rigidities are derived from.
  • model::M: the model the model column names, else NoModel
source

Models

Every component T{M} carries a model::M: the parameters a tool attaches to a row by naming a registered model in its model column, one extra column per field of M, in the units the model was registered with. A row whose model is absent or unregistered is a NoModel component. Reading refuses a filled cell in a column neither the schema nor the row's model names, an unregistered model's name included; strict=false drops those columns with a warning instead. A component reaches its model's fields as its own, so tube.EA reads tube.model.EA.
